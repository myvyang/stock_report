### P0010-P0011
- 原文章节：PART I；公司结构及风险因素（VIE安排）

- 重要性：核心
- 关键词：VIE, contractual arrangements, primary beneficiary, consolidated financial statements, foreign investment

### P0012-P0013

- 主题：承诺风险关联方, 公司治理
- 重要性：相关
- 关键词：PRC regulatory risks, data security, anti-monopoly, HFCAA, PCAOB, permissions and approvals
