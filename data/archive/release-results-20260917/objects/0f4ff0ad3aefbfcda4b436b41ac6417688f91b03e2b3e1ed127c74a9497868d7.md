- 重要性：普通
- 关键词：Power Equipment, NEW PRODUCTS, 电池, 储能电源

- 主题：业务与经营
- 重要性：相关
- 关键词：HOOVER, DIRT DEVIL, VAX, ONEPWR, Floorcare & Cleaning, 新产品

### P0056-P0057

- 重要性：核心
- 关键词：Review of Operations, Power Equipment, MILWAUKEE, RYOBI, Floorcare & Cleaning, 分部销售, 分部利润

### P0058-P0058
