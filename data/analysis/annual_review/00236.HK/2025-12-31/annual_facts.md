# SAN MIGUEL HK 2025 Annual Fact Pack

## Data Scope

- Company: SAN MIGUEL HK (`00236.HK`)
- Period: year ended 2025-12-31
- Source: HKEX annual report PDF, `2026033101438_c.pdf`
- Currency/unit in annual report: HKD thousand

## Business

The group manufactures and distributes bottled, canned and draught beers. The Hong Kong segment also distributes imported beer products and leases Hong Kong investment properties for rental income. 2025 segment revenue was HKD471.312m from Hong Kong and HKD266.009m from Mainland China. The largest customer represented 57.69% of group sales.

## Asset And Liability Facts

Total assets were HKD819.470m, total liabilities HKD122.911m and total equity HKD696.559m. Parent shareholders' equity was HKD717.292m and non-controlling interests were negative HKD20.733m.

Funds assets were cash and bank balances of HKD285.713m. Note 17(a) splits this into bank deposits within three months of HKD216.698m, bank deposits with more than three months to maturity of HKD11.072m, and cash at bank/on hand of HKD57.943m. HKD53.455m of cash and bank balances was situated in Mainland China and remittance out of Mainland China is subject to foreign exchange rules.

Interest-bearing financing debt was small: a related-company loan of HKD3.592m. Lease liabilities totaled HKD3.357m, but they relate to continuing operations and are tracked separately rather than mechanically deducted from detachable cash.

Investment properties were HKD94.527m and generated rental income of HKD54.310m in other net income.

## Profit

Revenue was HKD737.321m, gross profit HKD295.554m, operating profit HKD96.919m and profit attributable to equity shareholders HKD76.119m. Other net income included rental income from investment properties of HKD54.310m and interest income from bank deposits of HKD7.497m.

2024 reported loss included a HKD90.000m non-cash impairment loss on non-current assets and related deferred tax impact, so 2024 reported earnings are not a clean recurring-profit base.
