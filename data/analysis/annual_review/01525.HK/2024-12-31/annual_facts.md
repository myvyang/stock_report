## Business

The group principally provides higher education services in Mainland China. Revenue is mainly tuition fees and boarding fees, generally paid in advance before the academic year and recognised over the service period. All revenue and long-term assets/capital expenditure were in the PRC.

The group operates the education business through PRC affiliated entities under contractual arrangements. In 2024, those affiliated entities contributed RMB944.443 million of revenue, RMB171.225 million of net profit and RMB3,624.675 million of total assets. This matters for owner-earnback analysis because cash in the operating education entities is not equivalent to cash freely distributable by a Hong Kong holding company.
