## Business

GENCH EDU operates Shanghai Jian Qiao University, a for-profit private university in Shanghai. Revenue is mainly tuition fees, boarding fees, education related and training services. The 2025/26 school year had 24,182 full-time enrolled students. New undergraduate tuition was RMB42,000-48,000 and junior college tuition was RMB23,000.

Students generally pay tuition and boarding fees in advance before the academic year. The group recognises tuition and boarding revenue over the service period, and students are entitled to refunds for unrendered services.
