## Business

The group manufactures and sells lamps, luminaries, lighting electronic products and related products. 2025 revenue was US$222.898m, mainly from international non-NVC brand products, with additional international NVC brand and domestic PRC non-NVC brand sales.

Segment revenue:

| Segment | 2025 revenue US$'000 | Business substance |
|---|---:|---|
| International NVC brand | 30,471 | NVC branded lighting products outside the PRC |
| Domestic non-NVC brand | 8,716 | PRC non-NVC lighting product sales |
| International non-NVC brand | 183,711 | Non-NVC lighting products outside the PRC |

Major customer concentration is material: customers A, B and C each contributed more than 10% of revenue.
