## Business

Fountain Set manufactures and sells dyed fabrics, yarns and garments through vertically integrated textile operations. The 2023 annual report describes the group as a long-established fabric manufacturer with knitting, dyeing, printing, finishing and garment manufacturing operations.

2023 revenue was `4,440,235` HK$'000. Dyed fabrics and yarns contributed `3,639,699` HK$'000, or 82.0% of revenue; garments contributed `800,536` HK$'000, or 18.0%. Revenue fell 26.7% year on year, mainly because fabric sales volume fell about 22.9%.

Production facilities are in mainland China, Sri Lanka and Indonesia. Trading and representative offices include Hong Kong, mainland China, Macao, Vietnam and Japan.

## Cash Flow, Capex And Working Capital

2023 net operating cash flow was `293,758` HK$'000. Purchases of property, plant and equipment were `87,871` HK$'000, so reported operating free cash flow before further owner adjustments was about `205,887` HK$'000.

The 2023 operating cash flow was helped by working-capital release: inventories decreased `143,956` HK$'000, trade and bills receivables decreased `127,726` HK$'000, and trade and bills payables increased `165,429` HK$'000. This was partly offset by an increase in prepayments, deposits and other receivables of `27,547` HK$'000 and a decrease in other payables and accruals of `83,628` HK$'000.

2022 net operating cash flow was `905,634` HK$'000 and purchases of property, plant and equipment were `163,631` HK$'000, but 2022 was loss-making. The cash-flow pattern therefore reflects working-capital release during a downcycle, not simply stable profit conversion.

## Gaps

- 2021 operating cash flow and capital expenditure were not extracted from this 2023 annual report fact pack.
