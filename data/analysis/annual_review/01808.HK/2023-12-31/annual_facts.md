# 01808.HK ENTERPRISE DEV 2023 Annual Fact Pack

## Business

Enterprise Development Holdings Limited sells software licenses and other products and provides integrated business software solutions. In 2023 revenue was RMB60.646 million, including RMB36.251 million from software maintenance and other services and RMB24.395 million from software licenses and other products.

## Assets And Liabilities

Amounts are in RMB'000 as reported. Total assets were 302,764, total liabilities were 38,623, and total equity was 264,141. Parent equity was 212,888 and non-controlling interests were 51,253.

Funds assets were cash and cash equivalents of 177,805 and FVPL listed equity securities of 30,078. Cash included RMB92.126 million placed in PRC financial institutions, with remittance out of the PRC subject to exchange restrictions. The FVPL portfolio consisted of Hong Kong listed equity securities of 2,020 and US listed equity securities of 28,058.

Operating assets included contract assets of 22,936, trade and other receivables of 59,462, PPE of 2,419, intangible assets of 6,912 and right-of-use assets of 3,152. Contract assets rose because maintenance and other services were provided near year end before the right to payment became unconditional.

Financing liabilities were interest-bearing borrowings of 19,816 and lease liabilities of 3,247. Operating liabilities included trade and other payables of 10,628, contract liabilities of 4,809 and current taxation of 123.

## Profit

Revenue was 60,646, cost of sales was 34,601 and gross profit was 26,045. Profit from operation was 4,322, profit before tax was 3,404, profit for the year was 3,801 and profit attributable to shareholders was 5,550.

The profit contained non-operating securities gains: gain on disposal of financial assets at FVPL was 23,854 and fair value gain on FVPL assets was 330. The company also recorded loss allowance on contract assets and trade and other receivables of 7,314.

## Cash Flow And Capex

Operating cash flow was negative 12,537. Investing cash flow was negative 6,064 and financing cash flow was positive 92,882, mainly because the rights issue raised 90,653. Cash increased by 74,281 before opening cash and FX effects. PPE acquisition was 2,334 and payment for intangible assets was 3,854, so cash capital expenditure used in this fact pack is 6,188.

## Working Capital

Contract assets consumed 17,822 of operating cash in 2023. Trade and other receivables released 13,029, trade and other payables consumed 6,110, and contract liabilities released 2,724.

## Three-Year Cash Conversion

2021 parent net profit was negative 23,820, operating profit was negative 29,564, operating cash flow was negative 33,851 and capex was 2,294. 2022 parent net profit was negative 22,268, operating profit was negative 26,792, operating cash flow was negative 25,623 and capex was 2,167. 2023 parent net profit turned positive at 5,550, but operating cash flow remained negative 12,537 and capex rose to 6,188.

## Gaps

The annual report does not split parent-company cash versus subsidiary cash beyond the PRC financial-institution amount. It also does not identify maintenance versus growth capex, so maintenance capex must be estimated conservatively. Market capitalization comes from the rough screen historical signal.
