# AJISEN (CHINA) 00538.HK 2023 Annual Facts

Data source: Annual Report 2023, HKEXnews PDF. Reporting currency and unit are RMB'000.

## Business

Ajisen (China) operates Japanese-style fast casual restaurants in Mainland China and Hong Kong, with supporting noodle/product manufacturing and investment property rental income. Revenue was RMB1,815.406 million, restaurant operation sales were RMB1,734.200 million, and restaurants fell from 597 to 562 at year end.

## Assets And Liabilities

Total assets were RMB3,961.511 million, total liabilities were RMB903.585 million, and equity attributable to owners was RMB3,000.391 million.

Funds and near-funds assets: bank balances and cash were RMB1,607.635 million. Note 26 shows total bank balances/cash before cash-equivalent exclusions of RMB1,622.771 million, including RMB615.307 million term deposits, RMB15.136 million restricted bank deposits, and RMB992.328 million cash at bank and in hand. RMB1,249.634 million was denominated in RMB and subject to PRC remittance restrictions; US dollar balances were RMB295.415 million equivalent.

Investment assets: current FVTPL wealth management product was RMB25.031 million, non-current unlisted equity/fund FVTPL investments were RMB125.867 million, and investment properties were RMB1,007.255 million. Investment properties generated RMB41.525 million gross rental income and RMB401.228 million of investment properties were pledged as part of RMB412.382 million pledged assets.

Financing liabilities: bank borrowings were RMB37.484 million. Lease liabilities were RMB402.305 million; because these are continuing restaurant operating leases, they are treated in distributable cash profit rather than deducted mechanically from detachable funds.

## Profit

Revenue was RMB1,815.406 million, cost of inventories consumed was RMB442.076 million, gross profit was RMB1,373.330 million, operating profit was RMB126.842 million, profit before tax was RMB247.875 million, and profit attributable to owners was RMB181.188 million. Non-cash gains included RMB23.935 million fair value gain on financial assets at FVTPL, RMB4.947 million fair value gain on financial liabilities at FVTPL, and RMB8.822 million fair value gain on investment properties.

## Cash Flow, Capex And Working Capital

Operating cash flow was RMB503.177 million. Purchase of property, plant and equipment was RMB65.566 million. Lease principal repayment was RMB237.128 million in financing activities. Working capital movement was small in aggregate: inventories released RMB23.173 million, receivables used RMB23.712 million, payables added RMB6.870 million, and contract liabilities used RMB1.120 million.

## Gaps

The filing does not split group cash by parent company and operating subsidiaries. The separate company statement shows parent company cash of only RMB0.706 million, so most cash is at subsidiaries. The filing does not provide a maintenance/growth capex split. Investment property disposal tax and transaction friction cannot be precisely calculated from the annual report.
