## Business

The Group is a Hubei-based PRC branding, advertising and marketing service provider. FY2025 revenue was RMB290.937 million, split into branding services RMB116.672 million, event execution and production RMB65.152 million, advertisement placement RMB71.428 million, media partner rebates RMB20.089 million and online media advertising RMB17.596 million.

Revenue recognition and recoverability of receivables were key audit matters. Advertisement placement and media partner rebates are recognised on a net agent basis.

## Profit

FY2025 revenue was RMB290.937 million, gross profit was RMB180.019 million, profit before tax was RMB94.861 million, and profit attributable to owners was RMB71.356 million. Other net income was only RMB1.694 million in FY2025; FY2024 had RMB7.0 million government subsidy related to Hong Kong listing and no such subsidy in FY2025.

## Cash Flow, Capex And Working Capital

Net operating cash flow was RMB62.498 million in FY2025, versus RMB60.518 million in FY2024 and RMB23.887 million in FY2023. Purchase of plant and equipment plus intangible assets was RMB56.723 million in FY2025, RMB29.759 million in FY2024 and RMB3.866 million in FY2023.

Working capital is material. Trade receivables increased from RMB203.236 million at 2024 year end to RMB384.256 million at 2025 year end, and the auditor treated recoverability of trade and other receivables as a key audit matter.
