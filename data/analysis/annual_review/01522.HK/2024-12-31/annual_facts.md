# 01522.HK BII TRANS TECH 2024 Annual Facts

## Business
The group is an intelligent rail transit system service provider. 2024 revenue was HK$1,656.773m, mainly from intelligent passenger information services (about HK$755.9m), data and integration services (about HK$515.6m), and intelligent infrastructure (about HK$385.3m). Mainland China contributed about HK$1,596.2m of revenue.

## Balance Sheet
Amounts below are in HK$'000 as reported. Total assets were 4,833,116; total liabilities were 1,892,173; total equity was 2,940,943; parent equity was 2,775,601.

Funds assets: cash on hand and at bank 725,439; restricted bank deposits 35,765. Other financial assets were 197,387, consisting of non-current unlisted/FVPL/FVOCI interests in Cornerstone Huiying, Youdao Technology and Ruubypay.

Operating assets are heavy: inventories 451,136, contract assets 735,056, trade and other receivables 1,285,764. Note 19 says 28,262 of trade and bills receivables were pledged for factoring loans.

Financing liabilities: bank borrowings 101,507; other borrowings 283,262; lease liabilities 38,530. Other borrowings include factoring loans of 28,262 and a 255,000 borrowing secured by 30% equity interests in a subsidiary.

## Profit
Revenue was 1,656,773; gross profit 619,687; operating profit 201,875; profit attributable to equity shareholders 167,604. Non-operating or less cash-direct items include share of JV/associate results 21,888 and fair value loss on other financial assets 7,597.

## Cash Flow, Capex and Working Capital
2024 operating cash flow was 227,864. Capex for property, plant, equipment and intangible assets was 47,235, so post-capex free cash flow was 180,629.

Three-year comparison is weak: 2023 operating cash flow was 8,685 and capex 44,695; 2022 operating cash flow was negative 155,183 and capex 39,576. Receivables, contract assets and restricted deposits drove major working-capital swings. The annual fact pack therefore treats cash conversion as poor despite stable accounting profit.

## Gaps
The annual report does not split unrestricted cash between the listed/offshore parent and PRC operating subsidiaries. RMB remittance from Mainland China is subject to foreign exchange controls. Required operating cash and maintenance capex are not separately disclosed and must be estimated conservatively for the owner-earnback analysis.
