# 01587.HK SHINEROAD INTL 2023 Annual Fact Pack

## Business

Shineroad International Holdings Limited distributes food ingredients and food additives in Asia. 2023 revenue was RMB683.591 million, split almost evenly between food ingredients RMB344.256 million and food additives RMB339.335 million. Revenue is recognised when goods are delivered; payment is generally due within 30 to 90 days, while new customers normally pay in advance.

## Profit

2023 revenue was RMB683.591 million, cost of sales RMB569.129 million and gross profit RMB114.462 million. Profit before tax was RMB31.878 million and profit attributable to owners was RMB20.257 million. Important non-core or quality items include RMB11.031 million impairment on financial assets, RMB1.690 million loss on deemed disposal of an associate, RMB2.867 million share of associate profit and RMB2.600 million bank interest income.
