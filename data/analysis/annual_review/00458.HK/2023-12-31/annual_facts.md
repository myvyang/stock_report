# TRISTATE HOLD 00458.HK 2023 Annual Facts

## Data Scope

- Source: Tristate Holdings Limited Annual Report 2023, HKEXnews PDF.
- Period: year ended 31 December 2023.
- Currency and unit in this fact pack: HKD, HK$'000.
- Local filing: `/root/aicode/stock_report/data/raw/filings/00458.HK/2023-12-31/2023-annual-report.pdf`.

## Business

Tristate Holdings has two reportable segments: garment manufacturing and brands business. 2023 revenue was HK$4,215,667k, including HK$1,932,856k from garment manufacturing and HK$2,282,811k from brands business. Garment manufacturing profit before tax was HK$249,952k; brands business was still slightly loss-making at HK$4,587k before tax.

The brands business includes own brand C.P. Company and licensed brands including Nautica, Spyder and Reebok. Management stated that C.P. Company and garment manufacturing continued to contribute profits and cash flow, while licensed brands reduced losses but Reebok remained in rebuilding.

## Balance Sheet

Total assets were HK$3,116,678k, total liabilities HK$1,911,620k and total equity HK$1,205,058k. Equity attributable to shareholders was HK$1,180,201k.

Funds assets were HK$462,655k, consisting of HK$161,248k short-term bank deposits, HK$291,634k cash at bank and on hand, and HK$9,773k pledged bank deposits. RMB cash and bank balances were deposited with banks in the PRC and Hong Kong, and RMB conversion/remittance is subject to PRC foreign exchange rules.

Operating assets included inventories of HK$741,108k, accounts and bills receivable of HK$568,955k, prepayments and other receivables of HK$69,326k, and property, plant and equipment of HK$587,387k. Receivable credit terms are mainly 45-90 days. Inventories include raw materials, work-in-progress, finished goods and goods in transit.

Financing and quasi-financing liabilities included bank borrowings of HK$32,752k, all unsecured and due within three months, lease liabilities of HK$246,799k, and licence fees payable with present value HK$696,231k. Licence fees payable are contractual obligations under brand licence agreements and are central to the brands business rather than freely avoidable debt for a continuing-operation owner-earnback case.

## Profit

2023 revenue was HK$4,215,667k, cost of sales HK$2,432,652k and gross profit HK$1,783,015k. Profit from operations was HK$308,202k, profit for the year HK$180,173k, and profit attributable to equity shareholders HK$171,232k.

Non-recurring or quality-adjusting items included HK$46,055k impairment of intangible assets, HK$6,176k impairment of property, plant and equipment, and HK$3,125k interest income from bank deposits.

## Cash Flow, Capex and Working Capital

2023 operating cash flow was HK$471,489k after income tax paid of HK$118,115k. Cash generated from operations before tax was HK$589,604k. Payment for purchase of property, plant and equipment was HK$98,046k. Lease capital elements paid were HK$109,738k and lease interest paid was HK$9,886k.
