## Business

The group operates higher and vocational education, entertainment and livestreaming e-commerce businesses in mainland China. The listed company consolidates PRC affiliated entities through contractual arrangements because foreign ownership is restricted for higher/vocational education and TV/film production businesses.

CUCN had about 29,742 students at 31 December 2024. Education revenue is mainly tuition and boarding fees collected in advance and recognized over time. Livestreaming/e-commerce includes promotion income, commission income and artist management service income. The TV/film business still carries receivables and film-related investments with material impairment history.
