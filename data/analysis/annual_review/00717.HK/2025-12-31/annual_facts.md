# Emperor Capital 2024/2025 Annual Fact Pack

Requested period: 2025-12-31. Actual annual report period: year ended 2025-09-30. Currency/unit in annual report: HKD, HK$'000.

## Business

Emperor Capital is a Hong Kong financial services group. It provides global financial markets services, financing, equity research and corporate finance advisory services. 2025 revenue was HK$729.872m: global financial markets HK$540.663m, financing HK$180.392m and corporate finance HK$8.817m.

## Assets And Liabilities

At 2025-09-30 total assets were HK$5,924.629m, total liabilities HK$2,305.473m and equity HK$3,619.156m.

Funds and financial assets need strict separation:

| Item | Amount HK$m | Fact |
|---|---:|---|
| Bank balances and cash - general accounts | 2,253.844 | General account cash and bank balances, mainly HKD. |
| Pledged bank deposits - general accounts | 100.000 | Pledged to secure banking facilities; no bank borrowings secured at year end. |
| Bank balances and cash - segregated accounts | 1,834.290 | Client and institution money held in regulated segregated accounts, with corresponding accounts payable. |
| Assets acquired for financial products issued | 147.602 | Unlisted equity investments held as hedges for issued financial products. |
| Financial products issued at fair value | 147.602 | Matched liability against the above underlying assets. |

Operating and credit assets were material: accounts receivable HK$681.588m and loans and advances HK$877.393m after impairment. Loans and advances had gross fixed-rate/variable-rate receivables of HK$1,791.026m and impairment provision of HK$913.633m.

## Profit

2025 revenue was HK$729.872m and net profit attributable to owners was HK$146.936m. Profit includes:

| Item | Amount HK$m | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Commission and fee income | 495.788 | Includes HK$389.975m placing and underwriting commission. |
| Interest income | 234.084 | Includes HK$55.291m margin/IPO financing interest, HK$125.101m loans and advances interest, HK$53.182m bank deposit interest. |
| Loss under global financial markets business | -347.119 | Disposal loss on financial assets at FVTPL obtained from placing and underwriting services. |
| Net impairment allowances on financial assets | -43.205 | Includes HK$77.107m impairment on loans and advances and HK$33.902m net reversal on accounts receivable. |
| Share-based payment expense | -16.166 | Non-cash option expense. |

The five-year summary shows 2021-2023 losses and 2024-2025 profits, so a single-year profit run-rate is not enough evidence for a high-confidence sustainable cash-profit denominator.

## Cash Flow And Capex

2025 operating cash flow was HK$1,002.259m and capex was HK$0.387m. This is not ordinary free cash flow: the operating cash flow statement includes a HK$585.092m decrease in loans and advances, HK$356.213m increase in segregated-account cash, HK$344.502m increase in accounts payable, HK$160.000m decrease in pledged deposits and HK$234.688m interest received.

## Gaps

The annual report did not split general account cash by listed holding company versus regulated subsidiaries or disclose the exact liquid capital that must remain inside licensed businesses. For owner-earnback, these are handled through haircut and required operating/regulatory cash reserves rather than treated as freely detachable.
