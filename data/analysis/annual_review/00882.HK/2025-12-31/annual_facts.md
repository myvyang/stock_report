# TIANJIN DEV 00882.HK 2025 Annual Fact Pack

## Business

Tianjin Development is a Hong Kong listed holding company. Consolidated revenue mainly comes from utilities, pharmaceutical, hotel, and electrical/mechanical subsidiaries. Port services and elevators/escalators contribute mainly through equity-accounted associates/JV.

2025 segment revenue was HK$3.196 billion: utilities HK$1.264 billion, pharmaceutical HK$1.665 billion, hotel HK$136 million, and electrical/mechanical HK$131 million. Profit attributable to owners by segment was utilities HK$53 million, pharmaceutical HK$154 million, hotel HK$29 million, electrical/mechanical loss HK$75 million, port services HK$140 million, elevators/escalators HK$197 million, and corporate/others loss HK$70 million.

The pharmaceutical segment included a one-off special dividend from Tasly Bio-Medicine of about HK$305.076 million. Utilities revenue included government supplemental income of HK$102.126 million.

## Assets And Liabilities

Funds-like assets at 2025 year-end were cash and cash equivalents HK$2.811 billion, time deposits over three months HK$3.919 billion, structured deposits HK$449 million, entrusted deposits HK$1.107 billion, FVTPL financial assets HK$955 million, and restricted bank balances HK$70 million. Note 30 says cash/time deposits/restricted balances are mainly RMB. Structured deposits are RMB principal-protected products placed with PRC licensed commercial banks with 1-11 month maturity and uncertain final interest. Entrusted deposits are RMB deposits with one PRC financial institution, 6-12 month maturity.

Financing liabilities were bank borrowings of HK$1.981 billion. Lease liabilities were HK$15 million and appear to be ordinary operating leases, checked but not treated as detachable-cash debt in the owner earnback main case.

## Profit And Cash

2025 parent net profit was HK$428 million, but reported operating cash flow was only HK$46.8 million and capex was HK$66.6 million. 2024 and 2023 operating cash flows were negative. Associate/JV dividends received were material and recurring in cash flow: HK$397.9 million in 2025, HK$438.3 million in 2024 and HK$376.9 million in 2023.

## Gaps

The report does not fully split cash between parent/offshore/PRC operating subsidiaries. Time deposits are split current/non-current but no detailed maturity ladder is provided beyond the notes. Necessary operating cash is not disclosed and must be estimated for owner earnback.
