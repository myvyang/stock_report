Pursuant to Rule 13.49(6) and Rule 13.48(1) of the Listing Rules, the Company is
required to publish the announcements in respect of the 2024 Interim Results and the
unaudited interim results of the Group for the six months ended 30 June 2025 (“2025
Interim Results”) not later than two months after the end of the relevant financial
period, i.e. on or before 31 August 2024 and 31 August 2025, respectively and to
despatch its interim reports for the six months ended 30 June 2024 (“2024 Interim
Report”) and for the six months ended 30 June 2025 (“2025 Interim Report”) to the
Shareholders not later than three months after the end of the relevant financial period,
i.e. on or before 30 September 2024 and 30 September 2025, respectively.

As at the date of this announcement, the Company is still taking steps to resolve
the issues causing its trading suspension. As such, the Board announces that (i) the
publication of the 2024 Interim Results and 2025 Interim Results; and (ii) the despatch
of the 2024 Interim Report and 2025 Interim Report will be further delayed until
further notice.

The Board acknowledges that the delay in despatch of the 2024 Interim Report and
2025 Interim Report each constitutes a non-compliance with Rule 13.48(1) of the
Listing Rules.

FURTHER DELAY IN PUBLICATION OF THE 2024 ANNUAL
RESULTS AND DESPATCH OF THE 2024 ANNUAL REPORT

As at the date of this announcement, as the Company has not yet published the 2024
Interim Results, the publication of the 2024 Annual Results will be further delayed
until further notice. The expected publication timeframe of the 2024 Annual Results
is subject to the publication of the 2024 Interim Results and the progress of the
Independent Investigation.

Pursuant to Rule 13.49(1) and Rule 13.46(2) of the Listing Rules, the Company is
required to publish the 2024 Annual Results no later than three months after the end of
the financial year (i.e. on or before 31 March 2025) and to despatch the annual report
for the year ended 31 December 2024 (“2024 Annual Report”) to the Shareholders
not later than four months after the end of the financial year (i.e. on or before 30 April
2025). The delay in the publication of the 2024 Annual Results and the despatch of the
2024 Annual Report constitute a non-compliance of Rule 13.49(1) and Rule 13.46(2)
of the Listing Rules respectively.

Pursuant to Rule 13.49(3) of the Listing Rules, if the Company is unable to publish
the 2024 Annual Results within the prescribed timeframe, it must announce its
results prepared based on the financial results which have yet to be agreed with the
auditor of the Company (so far as such information is available). The Board, after
due and careful consideration, is of the view that it would not be appropriate for
the Company to publish the unaudited management accounts of the Group for the
year ended 31 December 2024 at this stage as they might not accurately reflect the
financial performance and position of the Group and the publication of the unaudited
management accounts could cause confusion and might be misleading to the
Shareholders and potential investors.
